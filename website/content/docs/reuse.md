## Open for reuse

The Dynamic PDB is built as a community resource for dynamic structural biology. Data released by The Dynamic PDB are available under **CC0**, including for academic research, commercial applications, and machine-learning training.

Files referenced in external repositories remain subject to their source terms.

## Acknowledge the resource and contributors

Acknowledgment is requested, but is not a condition of reuse under CC0. Please acknowledge The Dynamic PDB and the researchers whose data and models you use. Cite associated publications and dataset DOIs where available.

To make your work traceable, include relevant entry and model identifiers, external accessions, and retrieval dates.

## Tell us how you use it

We want to understand how The Dynamic PDB serves the dynamic structural biology community. If you use it in research, teaching, method development, or a commercial application, please tell us at prism-core@astera.org.

We welcome examples of analyses, tools, and findings supported by the resource, as well as requests for missing data, metrics, or functionality. Knowing what is useful and what is difficult helps us prioritize development around community needs.

## Get support or contribute

Contact prism-core@astera.org to request deposition access, ask questions, report problems, or discuss collaborations. You do not need a completed result to get in touch.

For technical issues, include the affected entry or model ID, steps to reproduce the issue, and any error message. Do not include authentication tokens.
